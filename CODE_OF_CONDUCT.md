# Code of Conduct

SoyaOS adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this community a harassment-free experience
for everyone. Be respectful, constructive, and considerate of different
backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to **conduct@soyaos.ai**. Maintainers will review
reports promptly and protect the reporter's privacy and security.
